# Three-Day AI Training Programme for SARS

**Course Title:** Artificial Intelligence for Revenue Services: From Foundations to Advanced Applications

**Instructor:** Professor Artificial Intelligence Expert  
**Target Audience:** SARS Employees (All Levels)  
**Duration:** 3 Days (24 Hours Total)  
**Format:** Interactive Workshop with Hands-on Sessions

---

## Course Overview

This intensive three-day training programme is designed to introduce, demonstrate, and collaboratively apply Artificial Intelligence (AI) solutions within the context of the South African Revenue Service (SARS). The programme progresses from foundational understanding and needs assessment, through practical demonstrations, to co-creation of advanced AI-driven solutions tailored to SARS operational requirements.

### Learning Philosophy

This course employs a progressive learning approach:
- **Day 1:** Assess, Understand, and Build Foundations
- **Day 2:** Experience, Explore, and Apply
- **Day 3:** Create, Innovate, and Implement

---

## Course Objectives

By the end of this programme, participants will be able to:

1. Understand fundamental AI concepts and their application in revenue services
2. Identify opportunities for AI adoption within SARS operations
3. Evaluate AI solutions for practical implementation
4. Use AI tools for predictive analytics and trend analysis
5. Collaborate on designing AI solutions for specific SARS challenges
6. Apply AI-driven analysis techniques to historical data
