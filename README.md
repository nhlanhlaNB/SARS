# Artificial Intelligence for Business Process Optimisation in Revenue Administration

Executive Programme for Process Designers & Engineers, Policy Makers & Operations Managers  
**Client:** South African Revenue Service (SARS)  
**Delivery Mode:** Face-to-Face, 3-Day Programme (08:30 – 16:00)

## Programme Philosophy

This programme is designed as an applied, design-led executive intervention rather than a conventional training course. Participants are positioned as technical experts and co-designers, collaboratively diagnosing SARS realities and developing AI-enabled solutions to priority operational pain points. The structure integrates demonstrations, critique, co-creation, prototyping, pitching, and strategic foresight.

## Programme Outcomes

- Validated current-state view of SARS technology and AI capability.
- Prioritised operational pain points.
- Co-created AI-enabled solution ideas.
- Future technology outlook for revenue administration.
- Grounded understanding of ethics, security, and responsible AI.
- Clear pathways toward pilot implementation and institutional learning.
