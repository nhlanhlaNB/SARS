Implementation Roadmap Guide

Practical pathway from idea to pilot and scaled deployment

Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–3)

  • Set governance, ownership, and project charter.
  • Assess data readiness and quality baselines.
  • Build delivery team and capability plan.
  • Finalize infrastructure and security requirements.

Phase 2: Proof of Concept (Months 4–6)

  • Define success criteria and scoped use case.
  • Build and evaluate initial model/system.
  • Run stakeholder demo and gather feedback.
  • Take a formal go/no-go decision for pilot.

Phase 3: Pilot (Months 7–12)

  • Deploy in controlled operational environment.
  • Integrate with target workflows and systems.
  • Train users and monitor adoption/performance.
  • Refine controls, quality, and operating model.

Phase 4: Scale (Months 13–18)

  • Roll out in phased waves across business units.
  • Drive change management and user support.
  • Track impact metrics and optimize continuously.
  • Embed long-term governance and learning loops.

Minimum Governance Checklist

  • Business owner and accountable sponsor assigned
  • Data governance and access controls approved
  • Risk and ethics review completed
  • Monitoring and incident-response process defined
  • Human oversight points documented
  • Benefits tracking baseline confirmed
