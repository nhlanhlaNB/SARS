International AI Case Studies

AI in revenue services globally and practical lessons for SARS

Singapore IRAS

Intelligent Tax Assistant (Ask Jamie)

Resolved high-volume taxpayer queries with NLP chatbot support and strong human escalation paths.

Denmark SKAT

Predictive Compliance

Used risk scoring models to prioritize audits, improve recovery outcomes, and optimize enforcement resources.

India Income Tax

Intelligent Document Processing

Applied OCR plus AI validation to reduce return processing delays and improve extraction quality.

United Kingdom HMRC

Fraud Detection Analytics

Combined network analysis and anomaly detection to identify sophisticated fraud patterns at scale.

Australia ATO

Predictive Analytics Platform

Built long-term forecasting and policy insight capabilities on historical revenue and compliance datasets.

United States IRS

Identity Verification

Strengthened taxpayer identity assurance using multi-factor checks and risk-based verification workflows.

Key Lessons for SARS

  • Start with high-volume pain points where impact is measurable.
  • Use human-in-the-loop controls for critical decisions.
  • Invest early in data quality and governance foundations.
  • Scale from pilots with clear success metrics and feedback loops.
