### Session 2: AI Solutions Showroom - Part 1 (08:30 - 10:30)

**Objectives:**
- Experience a variety of AI solutions relevant to SARS
- Understand the development speed and flexibility of modern AI
- See real-world applications in action

**Demonstrations Include:**

#### 1. Intelligent Document Processing (30 minutes)
- **Problem Solved:** Manual data entry from tax forms, receipts, invoices
- **AI Solution:** Automated document extraction and validation
- **Live Demo:** Processing various tax documents
- **SARS Application:** Tax return processing, compliance checks

#### 2. Chatbot and Virtual Assistant (25 minutes)
- **Problem Solved:** High volume of repetitive taxpayer queries
- **AI Solution:** 24/7 automated customer support
- **Live Demo:** Taxpayer query handling
- **SARS Application:** First-line taxpayer support, FAQs

#### 3. Fraud Detection System (25 minutes)
- **Problem Solved:** Identifying suspicious patterns in large datasets
- **AI Solution:** Anomaly detection and risk scoring
- **Live Demo:** Real-time transaction analysis
- **SARS Application:** Tax fraud detection, audit prioritization

#### 4. Predictive Compliance Tool (25 minutes)
- **Problem Solved:** Proactive identification of compliance risks
- **AI Solution:** Predictive modeling of taxpayer behavior
- **Live Demo:** Compliance risk scoring
- **SARS Application:** Targeting non-compliant taxpayers

### Session 4: Interactive Workshop - Adapting Solutions for SARS (13:30 - 15:00)

**Objectives:**
- Discuss how demonstrated solutions apply to SARS
- Explore customization and scaling requirements
- Identify implementation priorities

**Workshop Structure:**

#### Part 1: Small Group Discussions (45 minutes)
Groups assigned by SARS function/department:
- Group 1: Taxpayer Services
- Group 2: Compliance and Enforcement
- Group 3: Collections and Debt Management
- Group 4: Operations and IT
- Group 5: Analytics and Research

**Task for Each Group:**
1. Select 2-3 AI solutions most relevant to your function
2. Discuss specific use cases within SARS
3. Identify data requirements and challenges
4. Propose implementation approach

#### Part 2: Group Presentations and Discussion (45 minutes)
- Each group presents their findings (7 minutes each)
- Class discussion on cross-functional applications
- Instructor synthesis and insights
