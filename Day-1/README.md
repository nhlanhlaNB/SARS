### Session 5: Pain Points Identification Workshop (15:15 - 16:45)

**Objectives:**
- Identify current challenges in SARS operations
- Explore opportunities for AI adoption
- Prioritize problems that AI could help solve

**Activities:**
- Facilitated brainstorming session
- Small group discussions by department/function
- Pain points mapping exercise
- Prioritization voting
- Group presentations

**Workshop Structure:**
1. **Individual Reflection (15 minutes)**
   - What are your biggest daily challenges?
   - Where do you spend most time on repetitive tasks?
   - What decisions require better data insights?

2. **Small Group Discussion (30 minutes)**
   - Share individual pain points
   - Group similar challenges
   - Identify top 3 pain points per group

3. **Group Presentations (30 minutes)**
   - Each group presents their findings
   - Class discussion and synthesis
   - Collective prioritization

4. **Wrap-up (15 minutes)**
   - Summary of identified pain points
   - Preview of Day 2 solutions

## Key Learning Outcomes for Day 1

By the end of Day 1, participants will be able to:

✓ Navigate the AI platform and access learning resources  
✓ Understand fundamental AI concepts and terminology  
✓ Identify AI applications relevant to revenue services  
✓ Articulate their organization's AI readiness level  
✓ List key pain points where AI could provide value  
✓ Explain the difference between various AI technologies
