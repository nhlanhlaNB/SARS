Day 2 Schedule

Time Session Activities
08:30 - 08:45 Recap & Design Criteria Recap of Day 1 outcomes, clarify feasibility, legality and explainability criteria, and outline expected outputs.
08:45 - 10:00 AI Toolkit Summary State-of-the-art technologies for revenue authorities: decision intelligence, workflow orchestration, process mining, predictive analytics and generative AI applications.
10:00 - 10:15 Break Tea/Coffee break.
10:15 - 12:30 Co-Creation Studio I Map pain points to AI tools (apps/games), sketch architectures and workflows, define data requirements, and identify human-in-the-loop controls.

14:15 - 15:00 Pitch Preparation Structure problem-solution narratives, articulate benefits and risks, and define implementation considerations.
15:00 - 15:30 Solution Pitching Peer and panel evaluation, selection of top solutions, prize awards, and documentation of five solution concepts.
15:30 - 16:00 Reflection & Closing Reflect on Day 2 progress and close the day.
