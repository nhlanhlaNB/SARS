Day 1 08:30 - 16:00

Reality Check, Alignment & Shared Understanding

Understanding where SARS is today

Theme & Focus

Theme: Understanding where SARS is today

Focus: Sense-making, validation, shared reality, and demonstration of what is possible.

Day 1 Outputs

  • Shared understanding of current SARS AI reality
  • Validated operational constraints and governance gaps
  • Prioritised pain points for innovation work
  • Team formation for Day 2 co-creation challenges

Day 1 Schedule

Time Session Activities
08:30 - 09:00 First Dance! Programme orientation, objectives, co-designer positioning, collaboration norms, SARS AI platform prototype showcase, and introduction to LuthandoAI.
09:00 - 09:20 What AI is and is not Interactive discussion: “Speaking AI, let’s play a game!”.
09:20 - 09:25 Break 5-minute random chat break.
09:25 - 10:15 Applied AI Demonstrations Demonstrations of AI systems and decision-support tools with implementation lessons; examples include CIB Apps, AI for Credit Scoring, ECOWAS Big Data Strategy, and Dad AI.
10:15 - 10:30 Break Tea and coffee break.
10:30 - 11:15 Build Session Hands-on deepfake creation and discussion of possible applications for SARS.
11:15 - 11:20 Break 5-minute legs-stretch break.
11:20 - 11:45 Working Hypothesis: SARS AI Reality Present a working hypothesis of SARS technology landscape, AI maturity, development processes, and policy-operations-technology interaction.
11:45 - 13:00 Breakout Critique Session Validate and correct assumptions, identify gaps, and surface operational realities and governance constraints.
13:00 - 13:45 Lunch Lunch break.
13:45 - 14:45 Pain Point Prioritisation Structured discussion of operational pain points, root causes, severity prioritisation, and impact framing.
14:45 - 15:30 Team Formation Form multidisciplinary teams, allocate priority pain points, and frame Day 2 AI innovation challenges.
15:30 - 16:00 Reflection & Closing Day 1 reflection and close.
