Day 1 8 Hours | 08:00 - 17:00

Introduction, Assessment, and Foundations

Understanding AI and SARS Needs

Daily Objectives

  • Establish baseline understanding of participants' needs
  • Introduce the AI platform and course structure
  • Build foundational AI knowledge
  • Identify key pain points and opportunities

Daily Schedule

08:00
Session 1: Welcome and Platform Introduction

08:00 - 09:30 (90 minutes)

Create a welcoming learning environment and introduce the AI platform.

Activities:
  • Opening remarks and introductions
  • Course overview presentation
  • AI platform website tour
  • Viewing of introductory videos
  • Q&A session
Materials: Welcome presentation, platform tour guide, orientation videos
Break - 09:30 - 09:45 (15 minutes)
09:45
Session 2: Baseline Needs Assessment

09:45 - 11:00 (75 minutes)

Assess current knowledge and identify learning priorities using the Matric Meter.

Activities:
  • Introduction to Matric Meter assessment tool
  • Individual completion of baseline assessment
  • Group discussion of results
  • Identification of skill levels and priorities
Take Assessment
11:00
Session 3: AI Fundamentals - Part 1

11:00 - 12:30 (90 minutes)

Understand core AI concepts, terminology, and applications.

Topics Covered:
  • What is Artificial Intelligence?
  • Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning
  • Types of AI: Narrow, General, and Super AI
  • AI Technologies: NLP, Computer Vision, Predictive Analytics
  • Ethics and Responsible AI
Take Quiz #1
Lunch Break - 12:30 - 13:30 (60 minutes)
13:30
Session 4: AI Fundamentals - Part 2

13:30 - 15:00 (90 minutes)

Explore AI in revenue services context with international case studies.

Topics Covered:
  • AI in revenue collection and compliance
  • Fraud detection using AI
  • Customer service automation
  • Data analytics for policy making
  • International best practices
View Case Studies Take Quiz #2
Break - 15:00 - 15:15 (15 minutes)
15:15
Session 5: Pain Points Identification Workshop

15:15 - 16:45 (90 minutes)

Collaborative workshop to identify challenges and AI opportunities.

Workshop Structure:
  1. Individual Reflection (15 min) - Identify personal challenges
  2. Small Group Discussion (30 min) - Share and group challenges
  3. Group Presentations (30 min) - Present findings
  4. Wrap-up (15 min) - Prioritize pain points
Tip: Be specific! Include time spent, frequency, and impact.
16:45
Session 6: Day 1 Wrap-up

16:45 - 17:00 (15 minutes)

Key takeaways, questions, and Day 2 preview.

Key Learning Outcomes

By the end of Day 1, participants will be able to:

Navigate the AI Platform

Access and use learning resources effectively

Understand AI Fundamentals

Explain core concepts and terminology

Identify AI Applications

Recognize relevant use cases for revenue services

Articulate Pain Points

Clearly describe challenges where AI could help
